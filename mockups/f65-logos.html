F65 Lighting · app icon

Six logo directions for the app

Each one is drawn on the macOS icon grid (an 824-point rounded square on a 1024 canvas) in the app's own graphite and amber. Every direction comes with a one-colour menu-bar glyph, since the app spends most of its life up there. Pick one by its letter and I'll turn it into the real app icon.

Key F

An F spelled out in lit keycaps, the way you'd paint it in Studio. It says "keyboard", "lighting" and "F65" in one shape.

  • Still reads as an F at 16 pixels, where finer details turn to mush.
  • Amber on graphite matches the window's accent, so the icon and the app feel like one product.
  • The menu-bar glyph is the same 3 × 5 grid, so the two are clearly related.

What happens after you pick

I'll export the chosen icon as AppIcon.icns (16 to 1024 points, @1x and @2x), add it to the bundle in build.sh and Info.plist, and replace the menu-bar keyboard symbol with the matching template glyph. The glyph turns grey when nothing is streaming, just like the current icon.
